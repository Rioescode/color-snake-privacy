Color Snake — User Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Color Snake has no user accounts. We do not collect or store personal data on our servers. Your progress is stored only on your device, and uninstalling the game removes that local data.

Requesting data deletion

To request deletion of any data you believe we hold, email esriorio@gmail.com with the subject Color Snake data deletion. Please include your Google Play account email or another identifying detail.

We will respond within 30 days confirming deletion or that no server-side data was found.
