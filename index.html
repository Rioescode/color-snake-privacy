Color Snake Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Color Snake (package com.colorsnakeexit.game) is a single-player casual game. We do not collect personal information.

Data we store on your device

Progress, cleared levels, high scores, and best combo are saved locally on your device (for example with local storage). This data never leaves your device through our app.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads or ad trackers
  • No analytics or crash reporters
  • No access to camera, microphone, contacts, or location
  • No selling or sharing of user data

Children

Color Snake is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
